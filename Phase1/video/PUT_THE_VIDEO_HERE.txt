Phase I video
=============

Save the recorded video in THIS folder, for example as  Phase1_Video.mp4

What the brief asks for: a 3-minute video demonstrating the problem modeling, the approach
and the results. All team members should appear or speak. It is graded on being clear and on
having clear take-away messages.

The folder "preparation" contains what you need to record it:
  Phase1_Slides.pptx / .pdf          the slides (the spoken text is also in the speaker notes)
  Phase1_Video_Script.docx / .pdf    who says what and when (five parts, 3:00 in total)

Animated pictures of the four searches are in  ..\code\results\phase1\animations

When the video is in this folder, double-click  make_phase1_archive.bat  in the project folder.
The "preparation" folder and this text file are not put into the archive.
